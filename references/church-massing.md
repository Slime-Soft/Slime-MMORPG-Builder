# Church — massing spec

Transcription of the left-hand building on reference sheet 4, region by region.

## Transcription

**The nave.** A long stone hall with a steep slate roof, ridge running along its
length. Stone below, and its GABLE ENDS are half-timbered — cream panels with
dark brown framing — not plain stone. Buttresses down the flanks.

**The porch / transept.** A cross-gable projecting toward the viewer from the
middle of the nave's front, with its own steep slate roof, a tall pointed
STAINED-GLASS window in it, and an arched stone doorway under that.

**The bell tower.** At the right-hand end of the nave and rising well above it.
Square, stone at the base, its upper stage HALF-TIMBERED with two tall arched
BELFRY openings (bells visible inside). Capped by a tall slate SPIRE with a
cross on top.

**Windows.** Tall pointed lancets with coloured glass along the nave. A circular
ROSE window on a front-facing gable.

**Doors.** An arched stone doorway in the porch, and a second at the tower base.

**The churchyard.** A low stone boundary wall with square piers encloses grass
in front, with a stone path to the porch, gravestones, benches, trees and
bushes inside it.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **Long nave with a steep roof and HALF-TIMBERED gable ends.**
2. **A projecting cross-gable porch** with a tall stained-glass window.
3. **A bell tower at one end**, rising above the nave, with a **half-timbered
   belfry stage** and two arched openings with bells.
4. **A tall spire with a cross** on the tower.
5. **Pointed lancet windows with coloured glass** along the nave.
6. **A rose window** on a front gable.
7. **Buttresses** down the nave flanks.
8. Arched doorways, clear approach, nothing buried in a wall.

## Deliberately NOT built into this prop

The churchyard — wall, graves, benches, trees. Those already exist as separate
placeable props (`stone-fence`, `gravestones`, `bench`, `hedge`, `tree-*`), and
baking them in would fix the yard's shape and size forever. A stone path to the
porch IS included, since nothing else provides one at this scale.

## Scale anchors

Doorway ≈ 2.4 m, lancet ≈ 3 m tall, nave eaves ≈ 5.5 m, nave ridge ≈ 11 m,
tower ≈ 15 m to the belfry cornice, spire tip ≈ 24 m. Nave ≈ 12 m long.
