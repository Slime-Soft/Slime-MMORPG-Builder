# Bakery — massing spec

**Reference quality: LOW.** The only source is the small "BAKERY" icon in the
CRAFTING & SERVICES row of reference sheet 1 — a thumbnail, not a detailed
elevation like the tailor or church. What is legible in it is transcribed
below; everything else follows the town's established half-timbered style
rather than being invented as "bakery-ish" detail.

## Transcription (what is actually legible)

**The building.** A half-timbered house: stone ground floor, cream upper storey
with dark timber framing, and a warm TERRACOTTA pitched roof — the only
orange-red roof in the row; every neighbouring shop has slate. A dormer on the
front slope.

**A chimney** with smoke rising, at one end.

**A shop opening** at ground level with goods on a counter under a small awning.

**Goods on the ground** outside: sacks and a barrel.

## Built in the town's style, not invented detail

- A domed BRICK OVEN as a projecting mass on the left gable end, standing on the
  ground with an arched mouth and its own square brick chimney. The icon shows
  a chimney and an oven is what a bakery's chimney serves; a brick mass also
  gives the building the one silhouette cue that separates it from a plain
  house at distance.
- Bread and buns on trays on the counter; flour sacks and barrels beside it.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **Terracotta roof** — warm orange-red, not slate.
2. **Half-timbered upper storey over a stone ground floor.**
3. **A projecting brick oven mass** on the left end, on the ground, with an
   arched mouth and a brick chimney.
4. **A counter under a striped awning**, offset from the door, with bread on it.
5. **Sacks and barrels** on the ground.
6. Door clear; nothing buried in a wall; nothing floating.

## Explicit non-features

- No second wing, no scaffold, no tower — this is one modest block.
- The oven does not sit inside the building.

oven chimney ≈ 7 m, building ≈ 9 m long.
