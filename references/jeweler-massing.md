# Jeweller — massing spec

Transcription of the bottom-left building on reference sheet 3, region by
region.

## Transcription

**The building.** A two-storey block of pale ashlar STONE — squared, symmetric
and noticeably more refined than the other shops on the sheet. Corner quoins.

**The roof.** LOW and HIPPED — slopes on all four sides meeting near a point,
nothing like the steep gables on every other building here. Blue slate. Along
its top and at its corners runs decorative iron CRESTING: a row of small
spikes, with taller finials at the corners. That low hipped roofline plus the
cresting is what separates this building from its neighbours at a glance.

**Upper storey.** Tall ARCHED windows with dark tracery, separated by shallow
pilasters, over a carved string course.

**Ground floor, left.** A large projecting bay DISPLAY WINDOW, glazed, with
jewellery on show inside.

**Ground floor, middle.** A panelled double door with a moulded stone surround.

**Ground floor, right.** An open display area: a glass-topped CASE at waist
height with gems in it, and a workbench with small tools behind.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **Low HIPPED roof** — four slopes, no gable ends.
2. **Iron cresting along the ridge and finials at the corners.**
3. **Ashlar stone with corner quoins**, two storeys, symmetric.
4. **Tall arched upper windows** with pilasters between them.
5. **A projecting glazed bay display window** at ground level.
6. **A glass display case with gems**, free-standing at the front.
7. Moulded stone door surround; door clear; nothing buried in a wall.

## Explicit non-features

- No half-timbering, no gable roof, no dormers, no chimney stack feature.
- No awning, no market goods piled on the ground — this shop is tidy.

## Scale anchors

Doorway ≈ 2.2 m, display case ≈ 1.0 m high, bay window ≈ 2.4 m wide, eaves
≈ 6.0 m, roof ridge ≈ 8.0 m, building ≈ 8.5 m long.
