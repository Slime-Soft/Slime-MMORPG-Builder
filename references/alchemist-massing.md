# Alchemist — massing spec

Transcription of the bottom-middle building on reference sheet 3, region by
region.

## Transcription

**The building.** Stone-block walls, two storeys, pale grey. Gable roof of
slate, ridge running left-to-right, PLUS a cross-gable projecting toward the
viewer on the left half — so the front has two gable faces at different depths.
A red-brown BRICK chimney rises from the roof on the right. Small windows with
green shutters; a window box of green plants under one of them.

**The still.** The identity feature, and it is enormous. A tall apparatus
standing against the FRONT of the building, on the ground, projecting forward:
a broad brick/stone furnace box at the bottom with a dark arched mouth, a
bulbous COPPER vessel on top of it, and above that a tall segmented column of
pale green GLASS bulbs banded with copper, rising past the eaves to roughly
roof height, capped with a copper dome and finial. A copper pipe runs from the
column back into the building.

**Left of the door.** A tall wooden shelf unit standing on the ground, three
shelves, crowded with small bottles in bright colours — greens, pinks, blues.

**Right of the door.** A low bench/table with more bottles, some larger flasks
on the ground beside it, and green potted plants.

**Elsewhere on the ground.** Two or three barrels.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **Stone two-storey block with a CROSS-GABLE** on the front left.
2. **A brick chimney** (red-brown, distinct from the stone).
3. **A tall glass-and-copper STILL** against the front wall, projecting forward,
   rising to about roof height. This is the single thing that identifies it.
4. **Copper furnace base with a dark arched mouth** under the still.
5. **A tall bottle shelf** on one side of the door, a low bottle bench on the other.
6. **Green potted plants** and barrels on the ground.
7. Door clear, nothing buried in a wall.

## Explicit non-features

- Not half-timbered — stone, like the carpenter.
- No shopfront, no awning, no hanging bracket sign.
- The still is one continuous column: no floating segments (an earlier attempt
  built its condenser as a line of unconnected sticks over the roof).

## Scale anchors

Barrel ≈ 0.9 m, bottle ≈ 0.3 m, shelf unit ≈ 2.0 m, doorway ≈ 2.1 m, eaves
≈ 5.2 m, still ≈ 7 m to its finial, building ≈ 9 m long.
