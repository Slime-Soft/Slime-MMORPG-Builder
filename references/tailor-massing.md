# Tailor — massing spec

Transcription of the right-hand building on reference sheet 4. Region by
region, describing what is visible — not what the building "is". See
`blacksmith-massing.md` for why that distinction matters.

## Transcription

**The building.** ONE long rectangular half-timbered building — not two wings.
Long side faces the viewer, gable end to the left. Ground floor is grey stone
at the right-hand end and an OPEN SHOPFRONT at the left/centre, with cloth
goods visible in it. Upper storey is cream plaster with dark brown timber
framing and a row of windows. Two dormer windows sit on the front roof slope.

**The roof.** The identity feature. It is a PATCHWORK of individually coloured
shingle panels — muted blues, teals, greys and browns — several of them
carrying diamond/geometric motifs. It is not one flat colour.

**The sign.** A LARGE wooden board projecting from the left gable end on an
iron bracket, high up, carrying a pair of scissors. It is big — roughly a third
the height of the upper storey, not a small hanging plaque.

**Right of the building, on the ground.** A prominent stack of rolled cloth
BOLTS, piled in a pyramid, in many saturated colours — pink, red, blue, green,
teal, purple. This is the second-strongest identity cue after the roof.

**Left of the building, on the ground.** Two low tables carrying folded, stacked
coloured fabric. A spinning wheel (a large spoked wheel on a low frame). A
barrel. Front-centre, a table with red cloth spread on it.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **One long building**, long side to the front, gable to the left.
2. **Patchwork multi-coloured roof panels** — several distinct colours.
3. **Two dormers** on the front slope.
4. **Large projecting bracket sign** on the gable end, with scissors.
5. **Open shopfront** at ground level with cloth visible inside.
6. **Pyramid stack of coloured cloth bolts** on the ground to the right.
7. **Spinning wheel, fabric tables, barrel** on the ground to the left.
8. Grey stone ground floor, cream plaster + dark timber above, all free-standing
   props actually on the ground.

## Explicit non-features

- Not two wings, no L-plan (that is the blacksmith).
- No large chimney mass.
- Nothing placed in front of the door, and no prop intersecting a wall.

## Scale anchors

Barrel ≈ 0.9 m, table ≈ 0.8 m, cloth bolt ≈ 1.1 m long × 0.3 m thick, spinning
wheel ≈ 1.2 m across, doorway ≈ 2.1 m. Building ≈ 11 m long.
