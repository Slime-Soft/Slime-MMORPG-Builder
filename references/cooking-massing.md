# Cooking house — massing spec

Transcription of the "COOKING" icon, region by region.

## Transcription

**The building.** A modest block with a STEEP TERRACOTTA tile roof — warm
red-orange, with visible tile courses and a dormer on the front slope. Pale
plaster walls over a stone base, with some timber framing.

**Two chimneys, both smoking.** One is a broad STONE CHIMNEY BREAST on the
right of the front, running from the ground straight up past the eaves. The
other is a stone stack on the ridge toward the left. Smoke from both.

**The hearth.** At the foot of the front chimney breast, a large ARCHED opening
at ground level with fire glowing inside — an open cooking hearth, not a door.

**The porch.** An arched doorway on the left of the front, set in a projecting
gabled porch with a warm light inside.

**Right of the building.** A small dark awning on posts over a table.

**On the ground.** Pots, baskets, crates, a barrel, and food on the table.
A cobbled apron under it all.

## Identity features — checked one by one

1. **Steep TERRACOTTA roof** with a dormer.
2. **A broad stone chimney breast on the FRONT**, ground to above the eaves.
3. **An arched hearth with fire** at the foot of that breast.
4. **A second stone chimney on the ridge.**
5. **A projecting gabled porch** over an arched door.
6. **An awning on posts** to the right, over a table.
7. **Pots, baskets, crates, a barrel** on the ground.
8. Door clear; nothing buried; nothing floating.

## How it must differ from the bakery

Both have warm roofs. The bakery's mass is a domed BRICK oven on its gable
END; this one's is a flat stone chimney BREAST on its FRONT with an arched
hearth in it, plus a porch and a second ridge stack. Different silhouette from
the front, which is where they will be seen side by side.
