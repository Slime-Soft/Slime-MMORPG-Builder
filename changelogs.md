Update 0.13

* Guilds added — found one, invite players by name, kick them, and see who's online in the roster
* Character names are now unique per account, so a name identifies exactly one player
* Guild ranks are fully editable, with per-rank permissions for inviting, kicking, promoting, the bank and buffs
* Guilds can upload their own crest, which appears with the guild name above every member's head
* Character names now show above players in the world too
* Guild bank added — shared gold and items, with a recent-activity log and per-member contribution totals
* Guild buffs can be bought with bank gold: XP, crafting XP, damage, damage reduction and gold boosts
* New Guild Buff Builder for authoring those buffs — your own icon, price, duration and effect mix
