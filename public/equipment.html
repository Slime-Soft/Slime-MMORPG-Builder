Replaced Body Parts

A worn piece REPLACES the class outfit underneath it — leaving both in place puts two surfaces a few millimetres apart, which flickers in game. These are ticked to the standard set for the slot; add to them if your piece covers more.

Enchantment

Particles around the blade, plus a lit-up edge. Weapons and shields only. Style is what they are and how they move; behaviour is how the whole cloud brightens.

In the weapon's own space: +Y runs up the weapon, so height is how far the effect travels along a blade. Leave a box at 0 to keep measuring that axis from the weapon. Tick Emission box above to see it.
